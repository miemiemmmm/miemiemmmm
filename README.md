### 🔬 About me

- I work on **computer-aided drug design**, using molecular dynamics simulations and machine learning.
- I'm exploring how **physics-based** and **data-driven** methods can work together in drug discovery.
- I also compare oranges 🍊 with apples 🍎 and try to characterize the differences between them.

<details>
<summary>🍎🌳 <i>On apples, trees, and drug discovery</i></summary>
<br/>

- A string of apples 🍎 means the tree is likely an apple tree 🌳, but it is not always the case.
- It could be a basket 🧺 of apples hanging on a tree 🌳.
- It could also be someone having a picnic under the tree 🌳 with apples 🍎, oranges 🍊, bananas 🍌, and grapes 🍇.
- But if a tree shows apple blossoms swaying in the wind 🌬️, young apples maturing under moonlight 🌙, fledgling fruits enduring rain 🌧️, and gives off a rosy fragrance 🌸, you can be confident it is an apple tree 🍎🌳.
- Drug discovery works the same way.

</details>
